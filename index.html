Acute Medical Gas Services

Acute Medical Gas Services (AMG) was formed to provide comprehensive medical gas solutions with an emphasis on patient safety, quality service, and customer satisfaction. Our vision is to be a single-source, turnkey provider of comprehensive medical gas and environmental services.

Due to our in-depth understanding of the healthcare environment, we are able to offer our services with the highest level of professionalism. With over 85 years of combined experience, our team is among the most experienced and patient-safety conscious in the industry.

What We Do

  • Inspection, testing, system verification and certification
  • Environmental monitoring and testing
  • Consulting services and operation & management programs
  • Sales and installation of complete medical gas systems
  • 24-hour equipment service and preventative maintenance
  • System backfeed and shutdown management
  • Equipment rental and lease programs
  • Training and personnel certification programs
