Our training programs are suitable for ALL personnel who use, maintain, test and work with medical gas and vacuum systems.

Our classes provide individuals responsible for the proper use, handling, storage, design, operation, and maintenance of medical gas and vacuum systems with the knowledge necessary to conduct these activities in a safe, efficient, and effective manner.

Maintenance personnel, installers, design engineers, respiratory therapists, clinical staff, transfill operators and anyone else with an interest in medical gas and vacuum systems can benefit from our training programs. Training offerings include personnel certification programs, equipment in-service training, emergency operations and contingency planning, preventative maintenance and transfill operations.

Credentials & Standards

AMG is a training affiliate of the National Inspection, Testing and Certification Corporation (NITC) and the Medical Gas Training Institute (MGTI). Our curriculum follows the requirements of NFPA 99, NFPA 55, the ASSE Series 6000 standard and the applicable provisions of the Joint Commission (EC) Standards and the Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services.
