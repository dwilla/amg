Acute Medical Gas Services (AMG) was formed as a result of the merger of Certified Medical Gas Services (CMGS) and Green Mountain Medical Gas Services (GMMGS).

Since our team has over 85 years of combined experience, we have seen almost everything in the industry at least once. We pride ourselves on our “Gold Standard” operating procedures and our involvement across all areas of the medical gas industry, including:

  • Membership on code and standard technical committees
  • Publishing articles promoting healthcare education
  • Assisting in the development of Operation and Management programs
  • Providing training and consulting services

At any time, we — the owners — are available to assist you with any of your medical gas needs.
