With over 85 years of combined medical gas and vacuum system knowledge, our company is the most experienced, competent, and patient-safety conscious in the industry. The merger of GMMGS and CMGS to form AMG allows us the opportunity to be the region's leader in comprehensive medical gas services by focusing on patient safety, quality service, and customer satisfaction.

1978

Doug Ostraat founds Green Mountain Medical Gas Services (GMMGS). Doug was the company's president for 35 years and the company grew to become one of the most respected in the medical gas industry.

2007

Certified Medical Gas Services (CMGS) was founded by Jonathan Willard after working in the healthcare construction industry for numerous years.

2010

Medical Gas Certifications, Inc. (MGC) was formed by Jonathan Willard and Dan Chisholm, Jr. MGC was created to revolutionize the ASSE 6000 series of medical gas certification programs by providing the courses 100% online.

2013

Acute Medical Gas Services is formed with the merger of Certified Medical Gas Services (CMGS) and Green Mountain Medical Gas Services (GMMGS).

2018

Acute Medical Gas Services moves to its new office and state-of-the-art training facility in Goffstown, NH.
