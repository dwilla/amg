Training

Home/Training

Acute Medical Gas Services has developed training programs that comply with ASSE 6000, NFPA 99 and NFPA 55. These programs train and certify individuals involved with medical gas system design, installation, repair and maintenance, with customizable options available.

We emphasize convenience and satisfaction through multiple training delivery methods. Choose the option that works best for your team:

Classroom Training

ASSE 6000 certification programs are offered throughout the year at our Manchester, NH facility, with current schedules detailing programs, prerequisites and fees.

Learn more →

Online Training

Delivered through Medical Gas Certifications (MGC), our sister company. These 100% web-based courses operate 24/7 and eliminate travel requirements.

Learn more →

Customized Training

Onsite staff training at your location using tailored programs — ideal for instruction on in-house equipment at convenient times.
