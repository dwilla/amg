Online Training

Home/Training/Online Training

Online programs were created to revolutionize the ASSE 6000 series of medical gas certification programs by providing the courses 100% online.

The first online course developed was the ASSE 6040 Medical Gas Systems Maintenance Personnel Program, which launched on January 10, 2011. The ASSE 6005 Medical Gas Specialist and the ASSE 6020 Medical Gas Systems Inspector certifications are now offered as well.

No Time Constraints

You have 365 days to complete once you begin. Each of the ASSE 6000 courses is self-paced.

Available 24×7

All you need is a computer with an internet connection.

Technical Questions Answered

If you need clarification during the course, simply submit your question. Our ASSE 6050 Instructor is available for technical clarification.

These programs were developed by Mr. Jonathan C. Willard, who is an ASSE 6050 and 6055 Certified Instructor, and are administered through our sister company, Medical Gas Certifications, Inc. — the only training company to provide medical gas certifications utilizing web-based, audio-visual instruction and online exams to meet the requirements of the ASSE 6000 Professional Qualifications Standard.

The MGC education tracks were developed to provide individuals responsible for the proper use, handling, storage, design, operation, and maintenance of medical gas and vacuum systems with the knowledge necessary to conduct these activities in a safe, efficient, and effective manner.
